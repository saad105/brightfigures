Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Bright Figures LLC ("Bright Figures," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you visit our website or contact us about our services.

Information we collect

We collect information you choose to provide, such as when you submit our contact form or email us. This may include:

  • Your name and business name
  • Email address and phone number
  • The services you are interested in and any details you include in your message

Like most websites, our hosting provider may also automatically collect basic technical information such as browser type, device information, and pages visited.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your inquiries and schedule consultations
  • To provide, manage, and improve our services
  • To communicate with you about your engagement with us
  • To comply with legal and regulatory obligations

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with trusted service providers who help us operate our website and business (for example, hosting, email, or form-processing providers), and only as needed to provide those services. We may also disclose information when required by law.

Data security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please do not send sensitive financial information (such as Social Security numbers or account numbers) through our website contact form.

Data retention

We retain information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, or as required by applicable law and professional record-keeping requirements.

Your choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal information you have provided to us by contacting us using the details below, subject to any legal obligations we may have to retain it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at info@brightfiguresllc.com or (555) 010-0000.
